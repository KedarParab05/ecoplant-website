Plant Care Essentials

Science-backed guidance to keep every plant thriving

💧

Watering Wisdom

Stick a finger 2 inches into soil — if dry, water thoroughly. Most plants prefer slightly drying out between waterings.

☀️

Light Requirements

North = low light. East/West = medium. South = bright direct. Always match the plant to your window direction.

🌡️

Temperature & Humidity

Most tropicals thrive at 18–27°C. Group plants together or use a pebble tray to naturally raise humidity.

🌿

Fertilising Schedule

Feed monthly in spring/summer with balanced liquid fertiliser. Stop completely in autumn and winter.

🐛

Pest Prevention

Inspect leaf undersides weekly. Neem oil spray is an effective organic remedy for almost all common pests.

🪴

Repotting Guide

Repot when roots peek from drainage holes. Go up 1–2 pot sizes each spring for best long-term results.

✂️

Pruning & Shaping

Remove dead leaves promptly. Cut just above a leaf node at 45° to encourage bushier, healthier growth.

🌱

Soil Selection

Cacti need sandy, gritty mix. Tropicals love chunky aroid blend. Always match substrate to your plant type.
